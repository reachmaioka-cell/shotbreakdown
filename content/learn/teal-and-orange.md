# Teal and orange grade

Skin sits in orange. Skies, foliage, and many shadows sit toward cyan/teal. Push those complements and the frame pops. Push them too far and it looks like 2012 Netflix.

## How to do it without a gimmick LUT

- Start with a decent exposure. You cannot invent hue in crushed shadows.
- In the grade, rotate shadows slightly toward teal, keep midtones for skin, leave highlights closer to the source (sun, sodium, neon).
- Use a qualifier or hue vs hue on skin so faces do not go jaundice.

## When it is motivated

Night exteriors with sodium vs LED. Golden hour vs a blue sky. A tungsten practical in a daylight room. If both sides of the contrast exist in the lighting, the grade is finishing, not inventing.

## When to skip it

Period daylight, product work, anything that should feel observed. A clean rec.709 with contrast is often stronger.

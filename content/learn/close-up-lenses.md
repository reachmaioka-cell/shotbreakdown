# What lens for close-ups

Default: 50–85mm full-frame equivalent, around T2–T2.8. That range sits the camera at a polite distance and keeps noses from stretching.

## Why not 24mm

Wide close-ups are a choice, not a mistake — they feel in-the-room, documentary, or aggressive. They also distort faces. Use them when the distortion is the point.

## Why not 135mm

Long glass is beautiful and isolating. You need space, a quiet background, and a subject who can hit marks. On a real location you often cannot back up.

## Aperture

- T1.4: one eye, not both, unless they are square to camera.
- T2–T2.8: the usual close-up stop. Catchlight still lives. Ears go soft.
- T4: more of the face, more of the set. Use it when the room is the story.

## Sensor

Numbers above are full-frame equivalent. Super 35 50mm behaves like ~75mm. Phone 1x is often ~24–26mm — crop or step back rather than using 0.5x on a face.

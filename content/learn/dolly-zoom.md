# Dolly zoom

Dolly zoom (Vertigo / zolly): camera moves in while the lens zooms out, or the reverse, so the subject stays the same size and the space screams.

## How to actually hit it

- Subject dead-center, marks on the floor, 24–70 or 70–200 on a dolly, slider, or even a wheelchair.
- Start on the long end (subject fills the same amount of frame at the end as at the start).
- Move and zoom at matched speed. Practice once. The nodal point is near the sensor, not the front element — small errors show.

## What it means

Unease, realization, the ground dropping out. If the scene is a joke, the move will still read as horror. Use it once per film unless you are parodying it.

## Budget

A slider and a kit zoom can do a short one. Phones: some gimbals have a "vertigo" mode. It is crude. A real 3-foot dolly plus an 18–35 will beat it.

# Low-key lighting

Low-key means the fill is gone and most of the frame is below middle gray. It is not "underexposed everything." The key is still correct on the plane you care about.

## One source

A hard key from the side or behind. Negative fill (black floppy) on the other side instead of bounce. Background falls off. Practicals can be the only lights in the deep set.

## Faces

Expose the lit cheek. Let the far eye live or die on purpose. If both eyes go, the audience leaves. A tiny bounce card just for the eye is allowed.

## Grade

Do not crush to a black square. Leave a little air in the shadows so noise and texture survive. Add grain after, not instead of light.

## Night exteriors

Streetlamps and windows are the key. Your job is to kill fill (flags, distance, black wrap) not to add a moon. A "moon" 5K from a condor is a different, more expensive movie.

# Three-point lighting explained

Three sources: key (shape), fill (ratio), back (separation). That is the whole trick. Names of fixtures do not matter.

## Key

Biggest, highest, 30–45° off the nose. Soft for beauty and interviews. Hard for drama. Meter this first.

## Fill

Opposite side, one-and-a-half to two stops under the key for a classic interview (about 2:1). Kill the fill and you get 8:1 drama. Fill can be a bounce. It does not need to be a lamp.

## Back / hair

From behind, opposite the key, just enough to edge the shoulder and hair off the background. Flag it off the lens and off the seamless.

## Ratios you can remember

- 2:1 — commercial / interview
- 4:1 — narrative daylight interior
- 8:1+ — night, noir, "I cannot see their other eye"

If you only own one light, you still have three points: the lamp is the key, a wall is the fill, a window or practical is the back.

# Anamorphic look on a budget

Real anamorphic is a squeeze, oval bokeh, and characteristic flares. Most "anamorphic" phone clips are a 2.39 crop plus a streak filter.

## Cheap paths that still read

- Crop 16:9 to 2.39 and add a 1/8 Black Pro-Mist or a cheap streak filter for horizontal flares.
- Vintage spherical 35–50mm wide open: swirling edges, not ovals, but more cinema than a kit zoom.
- Actual budget anamorphic: Sirui/Vazen 1.33x or 1.5x on Super 35. You will fight focus and you will get the ovals.

## What you cannot fake well

The way backgrounds stretch when you pan. True oval bokeh on speculars. Don't add an oval bokeh plugin unless the rest of the image is already anamorphic.

## Shooting notes

Focus by eye; the adapter will lie. Stop down if the edges fall apart. Keep a hard source in frame if you want the flare — anamorphic without a streak is just a wide, slightly worse lens.
